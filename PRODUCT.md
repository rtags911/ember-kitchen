# Product — Ember Kitchen (fictional)

## What it is
A site for a fictional neighbourhood café with online pickup ordering. It must feel like a real, warm local café while stating plainly that the café and its data are fictional.

## Users
- **Commuters:** want to order in under a minute and walk in to a ready bag.
- **Weekend brunch / lunch runs:** browse the menu, check hours, order for a group.
- **Kitchen staff (/admin):** see new orders instantly and move them to ready.

## The one job
Get a visitor from menu to **placed pickup order**.

## Must stay true
- Menu, prices, hours and contact details are sample values, listed in README for confirmation.
- No payments online; pay at pickup. No delivery.
- Pickup times only inside opening hours.
- No reviews, ratings, awards, press or "selling fast" counters.
- Accessible (WCAG AA), thumb-friendly on a phone, fast.

## Tone
Warm and plain. Short sentences, food words over adjectives.
